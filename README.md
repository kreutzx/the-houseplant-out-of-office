## The line

> Out of office until spring. I am a fern: I have ignored 3 emails, 2 meetings, and one full watering this week.

## Why this line

One character — a fern writing its own out-of-office reply. It ignores work the way a neglected houseplant ignores care: 3 emails, 2 meetings, and one full watering, counted out in the same flat tone. The watering lands last, turning a standard OOO into a plant that has checked out until spring.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the line, plus a trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`, creating `/output` if needed.
